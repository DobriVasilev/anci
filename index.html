A letter of love
Dearest Anci, ♡

You might expect me to reach here for something dreamed up, something golden and soft. And I could. But I would rather give you something truer than any dream. I want to write of what is real. I want, even, to write of the hard days.

Why would a man put hardship in a love letter?

Because I have noticed something, and it has never once failed me. The hours in which I have loved you most were never the hours after a perfect evening. They were the hours after the hard one.

Strange as it sounds, it is the hardship that has made us. Every trouble we have ever met, we met as one, and from every one of them we came away loving each other more. This past month, the longest and cruellest we have ever been apart, should by all reason have worn our love thin. Instead we grew closer than we have ever been. We fell further into love than I believed a heart could fall.

Why? Is it only that we tried? Or is it that you and I were made to weather the storm, and that each time we do, we are shown once more that we belong wholly to each other?

I believe it is the second. And it is for this, above all, that I chose you. I understood long ago what love truly is. It is not the romance of the beautiful moment alone. It is romance in the plain light of the real, the very force that binds two souls to one road, one destiny, because their hearts have chosen to walk it together.

This is the love I mean, Anci. This is the love I carry for you. A love that endures the storm as faithfully as it endures the sun. A love so pure, so lovely, that it brings tears to the eye.

And so on this Qixi, while the magpies build their bridge across the sky, know that I am building mine back to you, day by day, word by word, and hour of work by the other.

Anci, I love you. ❤️ My beloved bunny. ♡ Your Bulgarian — Dobri
